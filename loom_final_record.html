Phase I: The Thermodynamic Promise

Optimization & The Geometric Scar

V5.0: The Jacobian Topological Tether

To solve Catastrophic Forgetting, V5.0 hypothesized that internal weights could scramble freely, provided the functional surface of the memory remained unwarped. By calculating a Jacobian-Vector Product (JVP) over a budget of 50 anchor points, we applied an elastic mathematical tether.

Result: On Split-MNIST, the baseline naive network retained 1.76%. The MEP Jacobian Tether retained 91.49%. It perfectly operationalized the "Lossy Geometric Scar" on simple Multilayer Perceptrons.

Phase II: The Reality Checks

Strict Crucibles & Convolutional Manifolds

V5.1: The Input-Space Falsification

Applying the Jacobian Tether to the raw input pixels failed completely on a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN). The network simply ignored the surface tension, scrambled its deep hierarchical feature extractors to learn the new task, and destroyed the old memory. Result: 0.00% Retention.

V5.2: The Latent-Space Falsification

Hypothesizing that the tether was attached to the "wrong end" of the manifold, we moved the JVP constraint deep into the Latent Feature Space. It failed again. Result: 0.00% Retention.

The "Toothpick" Diagnosis

A deep CNN contains millions of parameters. Constraining the topology at a strict budget of only 50 anchor points is like trying to hold down a circus tent in a hurricane using 50 toothpicks. The highly overparameterized network simply routed its internal geometry around the mathematical constraints, satisfying the penalty at those exact 50 coordinates while destroying the memory everywhere else.

As the Head learns Task B, it pulls the Tail through a highly viscous mathematical fluid. The geometry deforms gracefully instead of tearing.

$$ Tail_{t+1} = Tail_t + (Head_t - Tail_t) \times \text{drag\_coefficient} $$

Phase IV: The Hardware Manifesto

The Inescapable Reality of Digital Silicon

We subjected our theories to $L_2$ constraints, complex-valued phase rotations, continuous-time Neural ODEs, deep Latent-Space Jacobian Tethers, and Kinematic Viscosity. Every single mathematical attempt to solve Catastrophic Forgetting in software on a strict memory budget failed.

The Bottleneck: Parameter Superposition

In a digital CNN running on a GPU, memory is stored in discrete floating-point numbers within a shared silicon matrix. When it learns Task B, it must physically overwrite the exact transistors used for Task A. You cannot use software math to negotiate with a shared digital matrix. The medium is fundamentally destructive. Software algorithmic regularization is not magically exhausted, but it demands massive compute budgets and infinite constraints to constantly fight the underlying hardware physics.

The Horizon: Continuous-Wave Photonics

Catastrophic Forgetting is unnatural. In physical systems, information coexists spatially without destructive interference. In a fiber optic cable, a blue laser pulse (Task A) and a red laser pulse (Task B) occupy the exact same volume of space. Because they possess orthogonal phases and wavelengths, they do not interfere.

To achieve true, frictionless continual learning, the architecture must transition from algorithmic software constraints to physical hardware orthogonality. Task B passes through the exact same photonic cavity as Task A without requiring $O(N)$ derivative calculations to prevent interference.

The most important discovery of this project was a research process that reliably mapped the physical limits of software. We have proven the boundary. The theoretical pursuit of orthogonal physical degrees of freedom remains the definitive long-term direction of this Architecture.
